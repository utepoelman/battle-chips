Battle Chips: The Last Chip Standing - Rules

Free-Target Elimination Mechanic

All players deploy chips across board spaces. On your turn, roll the 2 dice:

  • IF you have a chip in the rolled space: You unlock a Strike! You get to select ANY opponent chip on the entire board to destroy! It does not have to be in the rolled space.
  • IF you do NOT have a chip in the rolled space: You miss and cannot attack on that turn.

Winning Condition

Eliminate all opposing chips until you are The Last Chip Standing! If an optional Round Limit is enabled, the player with the most surviving chips when rounds expire wins.

Ruleset Variants

  • Sum (2..12): Roll = Die 1 + Die 2
  • Difference (0..5): Roll = |Die 1 - Die 2|
  • Maximum (1..6): Roll = Max(Die 1, Die 2)
  • Minimum (1..6): Roll = Min(Die 1, Die 2)
